# From the archive: the 1975 dinner

This spring the family of a founding member gave the club a shoebox of colour slides. Most are of stamps. A few, labelled in pencil "Dinner, '75", show the tables at that year's dinner, photographed before the guests arrived, as if whoever held the camera knew the room would never look so tidy again.

![A long table with gold candelabra, tall candles and a fruit centrepiece](/_events/dinner-1975-candelabra.jpg)
![Lavender flowers and ivy around place settings and menu cards](/_events/dinner-1975-lavender.jpg)
![A round table with a pale cloth and gold chairs](/_events/dinner-1976-round-table.jpg)

The menu cards are too small to read even under a loupe, which has not stopped three members from trying. If you were there, or know someone who was, Constance would very much like to hear from you.
