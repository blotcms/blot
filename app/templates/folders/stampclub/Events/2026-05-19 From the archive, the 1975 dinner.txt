# From the archive: the 1975 dinner

This spring the family of a long-standing member gave the club a shoebox of color slides. Most are of stamps. A few, labeled in pencil "Dinner, '75", show the tables at that year's dinner, photographed before the guests arrived, as if whoever held the camera knew the room would never look so tidy again.

![A long table with gold candelabra, tall candles and a fruit centerpiece](/_events/dinner-1975-candelabra.jpg)
![Lavender flowers and ivy around place settings and menu cards](/_events/dinner-1975-lavender.jpg)
![A round table with a pale cloth and gold chairs](/_events/dinner-1976-round-table.jpg)

The menu cards are too small to read even under a loupe, which has not stopped three members from trying. The club had no home that year, between the stationer's and the library, which may be why it dined so well. If you were there, or know someone who was, Constance Whitlock would very much like to hear from you.
