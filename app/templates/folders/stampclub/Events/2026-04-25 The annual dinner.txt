# The annual dinner

The club turned seventy-five this year, so the annual dinner moved from the members' room to the Gramercy Room and its courtyard, and the dress code moved to black tie. Most people complied. Teddy Marchetti wore his letter carrier's jacket, pressed, which everyone agreed was the same thing.

{<>} ![Round tables under globe lights in the courtyard, just before the speeches](/_events/courtyard-dinner-at-night.jpg)

Dinner was followed by speeches, and the speeches by the toast. The toast is given each year by the longest-serving member present, and this year that was Inez Calloway, who joined in 1962 and has, she told us, been waiting a long time. It ran to twelve minutes. We drank to the founders, to Mr. Kessler and his back room, and to the penny post.

![Set tables seen from above: teal cloths, gold-rimmed plates and pink orchids](/_events/dinner-tables-orchids.jpg)
![A vaulted hall in blue light, guests at round tables](/_events/ballroom-blue-light.jpg)

The tables were cleared at midnight. The orchids went home with whoever asked first.
