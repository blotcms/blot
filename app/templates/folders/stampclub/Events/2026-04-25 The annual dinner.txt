# The annual dinner

The club turned seventy-five this year, so the annual dinner moved from the library's members' room to a ballroom, and the dress code moved to black tie. Most people complied. The treasurer wore his letter carrier's jacket, which everyone agreed was the same thing.

Dinner was followed by speeches, and the speeches by the toast. The toast is given each year by the longest-serving member present, and this year that was Inez Calloway, who joined in 1962 and has, she told us, been waiting a long time. It ran to eleven minutes. We drank to the founders, to the stationer on Fulton Street, and to the penny post.

The tables were cleared at midnight. The orchids went home with whoever asked first.
