# In conversation

We tried something new this autumn: no slides, no lectern, just two members on a stage talking to each other while the rest of us listened. Leonard Frisch has set type for forty years; Dr. Pruitt has filled teeth for forty-five. Between them they have looked at more small printed rectangles than anyone else in the club.

![Two people in conversation on a stage, the audience's heads in the foreground](/_events/auditorium-conversation.jpg)
![A quartet in warm light, the audience in silhouette](/_events/auditorium-quartet-warm.jpg)

They agreed on very little. Leonard thinks a stamp is a piece of printing and should be judged as one; Dr. Pruitt thinks it is a piece of mail and should be judged by where it has been. The audience voted, by applause, for both. The quartet that followed played something neither of them had heard of, which they agreed was the best part.
