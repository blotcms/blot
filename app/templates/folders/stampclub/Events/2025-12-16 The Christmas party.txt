# The Christmas party

![A reception room dressed for Christmas: two lit trees, garlands and yellow chairs](/_events/christmas-reception-room.jpg)

The tradition is simple. Everyone brings one stamp, wrapped, that they would be glad to see the back of. The parcels go round, everyone takes one, and by the end of the evening the club's worst stamps have changed hands and been forgiven. This year's prize for the least wanted went to a 1971 commemorative with a smudge exactly where the face should be. Its new owner has framed it.
