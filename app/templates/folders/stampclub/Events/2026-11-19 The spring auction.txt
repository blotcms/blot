# The spring auction

Once a year members sell their duplicates to each other, and to anyone else who turns up with cash. Lots start at a dollar; most finish under twenty. Oscar will run the room and explain, again, that the estimate is not a promise.

To enter lots, send a list to the address on the Join page by February 1. The full catalogue goes up here in late February.
