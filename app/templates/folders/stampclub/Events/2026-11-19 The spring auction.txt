# The spring auction

Once a year, on a Saturday in March, members sell their duplicates to each other, and to anyone else who turns up with cash. Lots start at a dollar; most finish under twenty. Oscar Bellamy will run the room and explain, again, that the estimate is not a promise.

To enter lots, register them through the link on this page by February 1, with a line on each and an honest word about its condition. The full list goes up here in late February, and the lots themselves will be on view at the stamp fair, on February 21. Viewing on the day starts at one.

Last year's results, and Oscar's thoughts on them, are in the notes of the May meeting.
