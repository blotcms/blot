# The juniors' table

From December, the juniors' table has a morning of its own: the first Saturday of every month, from ten until noon, in the members' room, through to the end of June.

Bring a grown-up and, if you have them, some stamps. If you don't, we have a box of duplicates, and everyone who comes goes home with three and a stock page to keep them in. We will sort, soak, look things up in the catalog, and find out where in the world each stamp came from.

No experience needed, and no question is too small. Grown-ups may stay and help, or sit at the back with the tea.

*Marguerite Fong*
