# The Christmas show and tell

The last meeting of the year is the one with mince pies. Bring one stamp, cover or oddity you found this year and two minutes to tell us about it. Ruth will keep time with the bell, and is merciless.

Guests are welcome as always. If you are bringing food, tell Inez at the door so she can stop people eating it before eight.
