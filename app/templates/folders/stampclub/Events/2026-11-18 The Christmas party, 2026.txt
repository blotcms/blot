# The Christmas party, 2026

The annual exchange is back. Bring one stamp, wrapped, that you would be glad to see the back of; leave with someone else's. Members and their guests only, and please, no more framed smudges.

The members' room holds sixty, and this year sixty people said yes inside a week. If you have a place and can't use it, tell Priya, and she will give it to the first name on the waiting list.
