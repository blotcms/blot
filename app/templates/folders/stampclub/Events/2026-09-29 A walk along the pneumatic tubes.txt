# A walk along the pneumatic tubes

From 1897 until 1953, letters crossed Manhattan underground, in canisters blown through tubes between the post offices at a speed that still makes Walter Dziedzic proud of a city he did not work for. He had planned a Saturday walk along part of the old route, with stops at the buildings the tubes connected.

The walk is postponed. Half the route is behind scaffolding this fall, and Walter will not lead a walk on which nobody can see the buildings. He is planning it again for a Saturday in the spring, and the new date will go up here once he has walked it twice on his own.
