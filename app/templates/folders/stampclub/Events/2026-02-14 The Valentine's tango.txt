# The Valentine's tango

Last year we read *Valentine's Day at the Post-Office*, the 1850 piece in *Household Words* in which two men post valentines and follow them through the sorting rooms. It left Constance Whitlock convinced that the club owed the post office a dance. This year it paid up.

![Couples dancing tango on a mosaic floor under marble columns](/_events/great-hall-tango.jpg)

A teacher from the dance school on Amsterdam Avenue gave us an hour's lesson and then left us to it. Nobody fell over. Teddy Marchetti, who carried letters for forty years, said it was the first time he had been on a marble floor without a bag on his shoulder.
