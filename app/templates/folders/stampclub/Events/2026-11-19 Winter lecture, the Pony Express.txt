# Winter lecture: the Pony Express

Teddy Marchetti, who carried letters on foot for forty years, will talk about the people who carried them on horseback for eighteen months. He has borrowed four covers that made the journey and will pass round photographs of the rest.

The lecture is free and open to everyone. Doors at half past six; the hall seats three hundred, and last summer it was full.
