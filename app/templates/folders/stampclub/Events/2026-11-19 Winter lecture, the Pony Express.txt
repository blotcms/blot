# Winter lecture: the Pony Express

The January meeting moves to Morrow Hall this year, and opens its doors to everyone. Teddy Marchetti, who carried letters on foot in Brooklyn for forty years, will talk about the riders who carried them on horseback from Missouri to California between April 1860 and October 1861, when the telegraph reached across the country and put them out of work within days.

He has borrowed four covers that made the journey, and will show photographs of the rest. The lecture is free, but the hall seats three hundred and last summer it was full, so please reserve a seat. Doors at half past six.

Teddy will end where the riders' route did, in the air: the airmail that crossed the same country in a day, and the flight that crossed the Atlantic in 1927.
