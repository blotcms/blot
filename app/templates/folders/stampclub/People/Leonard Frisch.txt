# Leonard Frisch

![D.O. Blood and Co. forgery, Leonard's favorite](/_stamps/2c D.O. Blood & Co. City Despatch Post forgery single.jpg)

Leonard set type for forty years and has edited *The Perforation* since 2004. As secretary he takes the minutes, reads the club's mail on Sunday evenings, and looks after this website. He collects forgeries of local post stamps, and judges every one by its letter spacing.
