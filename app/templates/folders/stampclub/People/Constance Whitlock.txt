# Constance Whitlock

![Universal Postal Union, Lady Writing a Letter, Constance's favorite](/_stamps/Universal Postal Union- Lady Writing a Letter.jpg)

Constance looks after the Society Library's maps by day, and the club's reading list on the second Tuesday. She chooses most of what we read aloud from *Household Words*, and she is the person the loupe goes back to. She prefers a stamp with a good caption to a good stamp without one.
