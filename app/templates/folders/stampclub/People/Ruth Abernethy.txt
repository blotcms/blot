# Ruth Abernethy

![1c Franklin, Ruth's favorite](/_stamps/1c Franklin single.jpg)

Ruth proofread bank notes for thirty years, and has been president of the club since 2011. She collects engraved portraits, which she says is a polite way of saying she collects faces. She chairs the annual meeting, keeps time at the show and tell with a bell, and answers the president's mail herself.
