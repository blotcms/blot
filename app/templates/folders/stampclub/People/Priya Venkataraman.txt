# Priya Venkataraman

![7c Stanton with a grill, Priya's favorite](/_stamps/7c Edwin M. Stanton grilled single.jpg)

Priya came to the club as a guest in 2016, and within a year she was treasurer. She keeps the dues and the kettle fund in separate books, sits at the table by the door at every meeting, and writes a receipt for everything. In her spare time she collects grilled stamps, which she says are about the same thing as accounting.
