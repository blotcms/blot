# Games, games

February is a cold month, so we held a topical evening on sport. Joyce Nakamura, who supervises the school's spring track meet and has a collection of Olympic issues to match, took us through the 1972 and 1976 sets.

She pointed out that the designers of the 1976 stamps used the same stylized figures, and that you can tell the summer ones from the winter ones by the length of the shadow. Leonard Frisch said that was a typographer's observation, and Joyce said that it was a teacher's.

The meeting spent a long time on the horse, which Oscar Bellamy says is an acknowledgment that the entire stamp business depends on betting.
