# Games, games

February is a cold month, so we held a topical evening on sport. Marguerite Fong, who runs the juniors' table and, at her school, the spring field day, took us through the 1972 and 1976 Olympic sets.

The 1976 set was printed as a block of four, and the fourth stamp shows a diver. Marguerite had left it at home, because, she said, it makes the block look wet.

She pointed out that the designer of the 1976 set drew the athletes in the same stylized way, and that you can tell the summer ones from the winter ones by the length of the shadow. Leonard Frisch said that was a typographer's observation, and Marguerite said that it was a teacher's.

The meeting spent a long time on the horse, which Oscar Bellamy says is the Post Office's acknowledgment that the whole stamp business depends on betting.
