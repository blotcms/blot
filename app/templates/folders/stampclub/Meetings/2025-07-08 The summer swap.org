* The summer swap

July is too hot for a lecture, so July is the swap. Everyone brings their doubles in a shoebox, and the boxes go round the table until the kettle boils.

** What changed hands

- A strip of /Lindbergh Flight/ airmails, for a run of *postage dues*
- Most of a box of Christmas stamps, for nothing at all
- One Graf Zeppelin, after a negotiation that took the whole evening

** Notes for next year

1. Label the shoeboxes. Two people went home with the wrong one.
2. Open the window /before/ people arrive.
3. Keep =the good stamps= out of the shoebox until the end.
