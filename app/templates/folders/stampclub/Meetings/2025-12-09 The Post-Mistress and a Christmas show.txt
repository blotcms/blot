# The Post-Mistress and a Christmas show

December is our lightest meeting. We read, we look at things, and we eat. Teddy Marchetti chose the reading this year: *The Post-Mistress*, from *Household Words* in 1855, a gentle sketch of a woman who runs a village post office and knows every letter in it by its handwriting. Teddy said that this is exactly how his own mother carried on in the Bronx, minus the village.

Afterwards the members laid out their Christmas stamps on the long table. The group is smaller than you would think: only a few Christmas issues appear before the 1970s, and members have strong feelings about all of them. The favorite, by show of hands, was the Currier and Ives winter scene, which two members said they had sent to the same aunt for twelve years.
