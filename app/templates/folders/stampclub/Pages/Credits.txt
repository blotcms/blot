# Credits

The New York Stamp Club is invented: its members, its meetings, Morrow Hall, the Gramercy Room, the Lorraine Hotel and the Hartwell Mail Museum exist only on this site. The Society Library is real; only the club's use of its members' room is invented. What is real is everything you can look at. The stamps are scans of public-domain stamps, the photographs of events are public-domain photographs standing in for the club's own, and the readings are essays from *Household Words*, out of copyright for well over a century. These are their sources, creators and terms.
