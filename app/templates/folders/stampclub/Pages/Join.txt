# Join

Membership is twelve dollars a year, payable in cash or, by tradition, in mint commemoratives of equal face value. A dozen one-dollar stamps will do; so will two sixes and a pair of fours. The treasurer is fair about change.

## What members get

- A seat at every meeting, and a vote at the annual one.
- Access to the club's shared album, which holds over a hundred stamps and is kept in the Society Library's safe.
- The use of the loupe, the tongs and the good lamp.
- A monthly note by email with the date, the speaker and the topic, and a link to the notes of the previous meeting.
- Two newsletters a year, assembled by our typesetter and printed on paper.
- An invitation to the summer outing and the December reading.
- A say in what we read.

## How to sign up

Come to any meeting and speak to the treasurer, Priya Venkataraman, at the table by the door. She will take your twelve dollars (or its equivalent in stamps) and write your name in the book.

If you cannot come in person, write to join@nystampclub.example with your name, your address and a sentence about what you collect, even if it is nothing yet. We will write back with the details and a place to send your dues.

## Good to know

- Membership runs from the meeting at which you join, for one year.
- Beginners are welcome, and so are people who stopped collecting in 1974.
- Students under eighteen join free.
- There are no committees you must serve on, though we will ask.
