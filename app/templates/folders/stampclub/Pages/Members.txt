# Members

The club has about sixty members. These are the ones you will meet first, usually because they got there early to put out the chairs. There is more about [the officers](/tagged/people), who were elected in March, on their own pages. Each chose a stamp to stand in for a photograph, on the grounds that it is a better likeness.

{||||} ![1c Franklin, Ruth's favorite](/_stamps/1c Franklin single.jpg)
    **Ruth Abernethy**
    *President*
    Proofread bank notes for thirty years and collects engraved portraits.
    Has run the club since 2011, and cannot pass a bad border in silence.

{||||} ![7c Stanton with a grill, Priya's favorite](/_stamps/7c Edwin M. Stanton grilled single.jpg)
    **Priya Venkataraman**
    *Treasurer*
    Came as a guest in 2016 and had the cash box within a year.
    Keeps the books and a drawer of grilled stamps, in that order.

{||||} ![Universal Postal Union, Lady Writing a Letter, Constance's favorite](/_stamps/Universal Postal Union- Lady Writing a Letter.jpg)
    **Constance Whitlock**
    *Librarian*
    Looks after the Society Library's maps, and the club's reading list.
    Prefers a stamp with a good caption to a good stamp without one.

{||||} ![D.O. Blood and Co. forgery, Leonard's favorite](/_stamps/2c D.O. Blood & Co. City Despatch Post forgery single.jpg)
    **Leonard Frisch**
    *Secretary, and editor of* The Perforation
    Set type for forty years and now sets the newsletter.
    Collects forgeries, and is only slightly embarrassed about it.

{||||} ![Graf Zeppelin airmail stamp, Harold's favorite](/_stamps/Graf Zeppelin airmail stamp.jpg)
    **Dr. Harold Pruitt**
    *Airmail*
    A dentist, and a member since 1994.
    Collects airmail and nothing else, which he calls a discipline.

{||||} ![Postage due stamp, Teddy's favorite](/_stamps/Postage Due Stamp (2).jpg)
    **Teddy Marchetti**
    *Postal history*
    Carried letters in Brooklyn for forty years, and still owns the jacket.
    Collects postage due stamps, with an affection he cannot explain.

{||||} ![Mineral Heritage amethyst, Inez's favorite](/_stamps/Mineral Heritage Amethyst.jpg)
    **Inez Calloway**
    *The door*
    Joined in 1962 and is the club's oldest member, by some distance.
    Taught science for thirty-five years; now minds the door and the visitors' book.

{||||} ![15c Landing of Columbus, Walter's favorite](/_stamps/15c Landing of Columbus G Grill single.jpg)
    **Walter Dziedzic**
    *Outings*
    Wrote railroad timetables for thirty-four years, and plans the summer outing.
    Collects the 1869 issue, and has never let a timetable stand in his way.

{||||} ![Horse Racing, Oscar's favorite](/_stamps/Horse Racing.jpg)
    **Oscar Bellamy**
    *The auction*
    Runs the spring sale, and has never once bid at it.
    Believes every estimate is a story told by someone who wants you there.

{||||} ![Wildlife Conservation, American bison, Marguerite's favorite](/_stamps/Wildlife Conservation- American Bison.jpg)
    **Marguerite Fong**
    *The juniors' table*
    Teaches fourth grade, and keeps the box of duplicates for new collectors.
    Collects animals, and will tell you that birds count.

If you would like to write to any of them, their addresses are on the [Contact](/contact) page.
