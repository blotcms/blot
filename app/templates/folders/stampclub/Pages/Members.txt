# Members

The club has about sixty members. These are a few of the people who keep it running, each with a stamp they are fond of.

## Ruth Abernethy, president

Ruth has run the club since 2011 and collects engraved portraits, which she says is a polite way of saying she collects faces. She was a bank-note proofreader for thirty years and cannot see a bad border without wanting to say something.

![1c Franklin, Ruth's favorite](/_stamps/1c Franklin single.jpg)

## Priya Venkataraman, treasurer

Priya keeps the books and the grilled stamps, in that order. She joined as a guest in 2016, and a year later she was in charge of the money. She says that was not an accident.

![7c Stanton with a grill, Priya's favorite](/_stamps/7c Edwin M. Stanton grilled single.jpg)

## Dr. Harold Pruitt, vice president

A dentist, a member since 1994, and a collector of nothing but airmail. He has been flown on every airline that still has a stamp to its name and is happy to tell you about each.

![Graf Zeppelin airmail stamp, Harold's favorite](/_stamps/Graf Zeppelin airmail stamp.jpg)

## Walter Dziedzic, outings

Retired from the Post Office after thirty-four years in Queens. Walter plans the summer outing, and has never once let a timetable stand in his way. He collects stamps of the 1869 issue.

![15c Landing of Columbus, Walter's favorite](/_stamps/15c Landing of Columbus G Grill single.jpg)

## Constance Whitlock, librarian

Constance looks after the Society Library's maps and, on the second Tuesday, the club's reading list. She prefers a stamp with a good caption, and a good caption with a poor stamp.

![Universal Postal Union, Lady Writing a Letter, Constance's favorite](/_stamps/Universal Postal Union- Lady Writing a Letter.jpg)

## Leonard Frisch, newsletter

A typesetter for forty years, now retired, and responsible for the club's printed newsletter, which has never contained a typo, he says, that was not intentional. He collects forgeries and is only slightly embarrassed about it.

![D.O. Blood and Co. forgery, Leonard's favorite](/_stamps/2c D.O. Blood & Co. City Despatch Post forgery single.jpg)

## Joyce Nakamura, young collectors

Joyce teaches fourth grade and runs the club's junior table. She collects animals and, as she will tell you, birds count.

![Wildlife Conservation, American bison, Joyce's favorite](/_stamps/Wildlife Conservation- American Bison.jpg)

## Teddy Marchetti, postal history

A retired letter carrier of forty years, and the member most likely to know what happened to your letter. He collects postage due stamps, with an affection he cannot explain.

![Postage due stamp, Teddy's favorite](/_stamps/Postage Due Stamp (2).jpg)
