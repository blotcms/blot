# Contact

The quickest way to reach the club is to come on a second Tuesday. The next quickest is to write to hello@nystampclub.example, which Leonard reads on Sunday evenings. If you know who you want, write to them directly; there is more about [the officers](/tagged/people) on their own pages. The phone above is an answering machine in Leonard's hall, and he returns calls on Sundays too.
