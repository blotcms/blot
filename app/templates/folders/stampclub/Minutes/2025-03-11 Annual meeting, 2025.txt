# Annual meeting, 2025

*Minutes of the annual meeting of the New York Stamp Club, held in the members' room of the Society Library on Tuesday, March 11, 2025, at 6:30 pm.*

**Present:** 34 members. **In the chair:** Ruth Abernethy, president. **Apologies:** none, which the secretary records because it has not happened before.

1. **The minutes** of the 2024 meeting were approved.
2. **The treasurer's report.** Priya Venkataraman reported a small surplus on dues and a larger one in the kettle fund, after a good auction. She proposed buying a new urn. Carried, 33 to 1. The member against said he liked the old urn, and was told he could visit it.
3. **The stamp fair.** Walter Dziedzic proposed that the fair move from April to February, when members have less to do and dealers more to sell. Carried, without dissent.
4. **The summer lecture.** The president reported that Morrow Hall had been booked for June 17, and that it comes with a string quartet whether we want one or not.
5. **Any other business.** Inez Calloway asked that the radiator by the window be looked at. The president said it had been looked at twice. Mrs. Calloway said she was aware of that.

The meeting closed at 7:04 pm.
