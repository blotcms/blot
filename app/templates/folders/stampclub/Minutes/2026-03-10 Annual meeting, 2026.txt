*Minutes of the annual meeting of the New York Stamp Club, held in the members' room of the Society Library on Tuesday, March 10, 2026, at 6:30 pm.*

**Present:** 33 members. **In the chair:** Ruth Abernethy, president. **Apologies:** Oscar Bellamy, who was arranging lots for Saturday's auction, and sent his apologies by Walter.

1. **The minutes** of the 2025 meeting were approved.
2. **The treasurer's report.** Priya Venkataraman presented [the accounts for the year (PDF)](/_files/accounts-2026.pdf), examined by Oscar Bellamy. Both accounts are in credit. A member asked why the juniors' dues appear as an expense. The treasurer explained that the kettle fund pays them. The member said that was very decent. The accounts were approved.

    | Year to February 28 | Dues | Kettle fund |
    | --- | ---: | ---: |
    | Brought forward | $412.50 | $412.15 |
    | Received | $648.00 | $611.75 |
    | Spent | $788.40 | $648.99 |
    | Carried forward | $272.10 | $374.91 |

3. **Elections.** The four officers were re-elected unopposed for two years.
4. **Amendment to Article VII of the [rules](/rules):** that the loupe go round the room clockwise and return to the librarian. Proposed by Constance Whitlock, seconded by Teddy Marchetti. An amendment from the floor, that it go counter-clockwise, was defeated. The motion was carried, 31 to 2.
5. **Any other business.** None. Inez Calloway reported that the radiator had been looked at, and that it was now too hot.

The meeting closed at 6:52 pm, so that the reading could begin.
