# Annual meeting, 2024

*Minutes of the annual meeting of the New York Stamp Club, held in the members' room of the Society Library on Tuesday, March 12, 2024, at 6:30 pm.*

**Present:** 29 members, and two guests, who were asked to sit at the back and did. **In the chair:** Ruth Abernethy, president. **Apologies:** Dr. Harold Pruitt, who was at an airmail fair in Ohio and sent a postcard to prove it.

1. **The minutes** of the 2023 meeting were approved, with one correction: the kettle was described as "new", and it is not.
2. **The treasurer's report.** Priya Venkataraman reported that the dues account is in credit and the kettle fund is in good health. Asked what "good health" meant, she said it meant the fund could buy a kettle. The report was accepted.
3. **Elections.** Ruth Abernethy (president), Priya Venkataraman (treasurer), Leonard Frisch (secretary) and Constance Whitlock (librarian) were re-elected unopposed. The president thanked the meeting. The meeting thanked the president.
4. **Motion:** that cookies be banned from the long table. Proposed by Constance Whitlock. After discussion, the proposer withdrew the motion in exchange for an assurance that cookies would not be eaten over the albums. The assurance was given.
5. **Any other business.** Inez Calloway asked that the radiator by the window be looked at. The president said it had been looked at. Mrs. Calloway asked that it be looked at again.

The meeting closed at 7:01 pm.
