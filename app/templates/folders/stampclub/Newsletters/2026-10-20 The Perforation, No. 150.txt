# The Perforation, No. 150

*The newsletter of the New York Stamp Club. Fall 2026.*

**One hundred and fifty.** This is the hundred-and-fiftieth *Perforation*. The first, in 1952, was one side of a sheet and announced that the kettle leaked. We have come some way.

**The winter program.** Dates are firm unless the library tells us otherwise.

| Date | What | Where |

| Tuesday, December 8 | The Christmas show and tell | The members' room |

| Tuesday, January 12 | Teddy Marchetti on the Pony Express, open to all | Morrow Hall |
| February 20 and 21 | The stamp fair | The Gramercy Room |
| Saturday, March 13 | The spring auction | The Gramercy Room |

**The walk.** Walter Dziedzic's walk along the old pneumatic mail tubes, planned for October 24, is postponed until the spring, because half of the route is behind scaffolding. Walter regrets this more than anyone.

**Renewals.** Dues for 2027 are still twelve dollars. Priya will be at the table by the door from the December meeting.
