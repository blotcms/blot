# The Perforation, No. 149

*The newsletter of the New York Stamp Club. Spring 2026.*

**Seventy-five.** The club turns seventy-five this month, and the annual dinner on April 25 is black tie. Teddy Marchetti has asked whether his letter carrier's jacket qualifies. The committee has decided not to decide.

**The auction.** The spring sale on March 14 raised more for the kettle fund than any since 2019, mostly on the strength of a shoebox. Oscar Bellamy will explain what this means at the May meeting, and what it does not.

**The rules.** At the annual meeting on March 10, Article VII was amended to say that the loupe goes round clockwise and returns to the librarian. The vote was 31 to 2. The two have asked that their objection be recorded, and it has been, here.

**From the editor.** Leonard Frisch has set this newsletter since 2004. He would like it known that the misprint in No. 148, where the kettle "holds two more cpus", was not one of his intentional ones, and he has had words with himself.
