# The kettle fund, 2027

The kettle fund pays for everything dues don't: the tea, the juniors' stock pages and dues, and the deposit on Morrow Hall for the summer lecture. Next year it needs six hundred dollars, about what it raised last year.

The hat has done well this fall, and the fund has four hundred and ten dollars already. The rest usually arrives in December, in the hat at the show and tell, and in cards that say "for the kettle".

You can give at any meeting, by check to the address on the [Contact](/contact) page, or through the link on this page. What each amount buys is on the [Support](/support) page.
