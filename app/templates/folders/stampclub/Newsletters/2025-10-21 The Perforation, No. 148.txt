# The Perforation, No. 148

*The newsletter of the New York Stamp Club. Fall 2025.*

**The kettle.** The kettle bought in 1987 has been retired, with thanks, after it began to whistle in a key that Dr. Pruitt says he could hear in his fillings. Its successor was bought from the kettle fund and holds two more cpus. Please do not ask it to hold three more.

**The loupe.** The good loupe went round the room on September 9 and did not come back. It has a brass rim and a scratch on one side. If it is in your coat, put it in Constance's tray at the next meeting and nothing more will be said.

**Hinges.** Several members have asked Constance Whitlock to repeat her talk on looking after stamps. She will, in January. In the meantime she asks that nobody bring a stamp mounted with tape to the long table, because she will see it, and she will say so.

**Coming up.** Walter Dziedzic on Columbus, November 11. The Christmas show and tell, December 9. The Christmas party, December 16, with the exchange.

*Set by hand, more or less. Errors are intentional.*
