# Membership renewals for 2027

Dues for 2027 are twelve dollars, the same as they have been since 1993. Priya Venkataraman will be at the table by the door from the Christmas show and tell on December 8, and at every meeting after that until she has everyone.

Membership runs for the calendar year, so everyone renews now, except anyone who joined after September 2026, who is already paid up to the end of 2027. You can pay in cash or by check made out to the New York Stamp Club. Members under eighteen pay nothing, and the kettle fund covers them.

If you can't come in person, send a check to the address on the [Contact](/contact) page. The details of what membership includes are on the [Join](/join) page.
