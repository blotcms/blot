# New in the library

Three albums came to the library this summer, all from the estate of a member who joined in 1958 and never missed a show and tell. His family asked that they be kept together and used, not kept in a cupboard. They are on the bottom shelf by the window.

{>|} Albums may be borrowed for one month. Sign the book on the librarian's desk.

The first is a run of the 1960s and 70s commemoratives, mint and in blocks, mounted with a care that puts most of us to shame. The second is wildlife, which he collected for his grandchildren. The third is the odd one: forgeries, every page labelled in red ink, which he bought on purpose to teach himself what to look for.

## The wildlife album

{<} ![California condor](/_stamps/Wildlife Conservation California Condor.jpg)

The wildlife album is arranged by animal rather than by date, which makes it the best thing in the library to show a child. The condor, the alligator and the polar bear share a page; the trout and the king salmon have one to themselves. A note inside the cover asks that the juniors' table be allowed to use it, and it will be.

{>} ![American bison](/_stamps/Wildlife Conservation- American Bison.jpg)

The bison is the stamp most often asked about. It is the 1970 issue, and the album has a block of four and a single with a curious smudge in the corner that he believed was a printing flaw and the rest of us think is jam.

## The commemoratives

{<<} ![Declaration of Independence, strip of four](/_stamps/Declaration of Independence, strip of four.jpg)

The commemoratives fill two volumes. The strip of four from the Declaration of Independence issue of 1976 is the centrepiece and, at his request, the page it is on is the one the album falls open at.

{>>} ![Botanical Congress, set of four](/_stamps/XIth International Botanical Congress, set of four.jpg)

Opposite it, the botanical set of 1969, mounted with a pressed leaf from his garden which the conservator at the Society Library has told us we must remove, and which we have not had the heart to.

## The forgeries

{|<} Ask at the desk for the cotton gloves before you handle the forgeries album. Some of its pages are older than the club.

The forgeries album deserves a meeting of its own and will get one in the spring. Until then it stays in the locked case, and I have the key.
