# Getting ready for the show and tell

The [[The Christmas show and tell|Christmas show and tell]] is on ~~Thursday 10 December~~ **Tuesday 8 December**, the second Tuesday as always. The last bulletin had the wrong day, and three of you have already told me so.

## Before you come

Ruth asked for a list. Here it is, with what the committee has already done ticked off:

- [x] Book the members' room
- [x] Order the mince pies
- [ ] Choose **one** stamp, cover or oddity
- [ ] Put it in a sleeve or a mount, not loose in an envelope
- [ ] Write its name and your own on a slip of card
- [ ] Practise saying it all in two minutes

## The running order

The evening runs in the same order as every year:

i. Mince pies, and the kettle
ii. The show and tell, in the order you arrive
iii. The bring and buy table
iv. The vote for the best find

Ruth keeps time with the bell, and the vote works [[#How the vote works|as below]]. After the bell you have to stop, though you may finish your sentence if it is a short one.

## How the vote works

We vote by putting a penny in a cup beside each find. The rules have it as rule 9 onwards, which for the avoidance of doubt says:

 9) Each member present has one vote.
10) Guests may vote, but not for themselves.
11) The President counts the pennies and her count is final.

The winner chooses the first mince pie next year and keeps the Penny Black block of four[^block] in their album until the next show and tell. The prizes, from first to third:

A.  The block of four, for a year
B.  A packet of mounts from Constance's own drawer
C.  A year's subscription to *The Perforation*

[^block]: A facsimile, framed. The real one is in a museum and has not been offered to us.

## Questions we have been asked

> [!question]- Can I bring more than one thing?
> One. If you cannot choose, bring the one with the better story.

> [!question]- What if my find is not a stamp?
> Postcards, covers, postmarks, a letter scale, a rubber stamp from a post office that has closed: all have won before. The rule is only that it went through the post, or helped something to.

> [!success] Already found the best thing of the year?
> Tell [Leonard](mailto:secretary@nystampclub.example) what it is, and he will put you on first.

> [!danger] Please don't
> Bring anything you would mind a child touching. The juniors' table comes to the show and tell too.

The [[Rules|club's rules]] still apply on the night, mince pies or no mince pies. See you on the 8th.
