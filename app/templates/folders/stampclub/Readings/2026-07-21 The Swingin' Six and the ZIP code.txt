# The Swingin' Six and the ZIP code

ZIP codes began on 1 July 1963. To explain five-digit numbers to a country that had been happily writing "New York 21, N.Y." for twenty years, the Post Office Department made a short film with a band, the Swingin' Six, singing their way through the reasons. It is a government film and so belongs to everybody, and it is the most cheerful thing in the history of sorting.
