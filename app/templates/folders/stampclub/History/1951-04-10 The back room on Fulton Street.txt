# The back room on Fulton Street

On the second Tuesday of April 1951, Mr. Alvin Kessler, stationer, of Fulton Street, gave the eleven people who kept asking about the new commemoratives the use of his back room, on condition that they buy their mounts from him. They called themselves the New York Stamp Club before the kettle had boiled.

![3c Washington, the stamp on most of the club's first letters](/_stamps/3c Washington single.jpg)

The first minute records the purchase of the kettle. The second records that it leaked.
