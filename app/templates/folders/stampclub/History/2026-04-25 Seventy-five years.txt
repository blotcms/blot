# Seventy-five years

The club turned seventy-five in the spring of 2026 and celebrated with its first black-tie dinner, at the Gramercy Room. About ninety members and guests came. Inez Calloway, a member since 1962, gave the toast, which ran to twelve minutes and broke her own record.
