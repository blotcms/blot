# The first exhibition

In November 1962 the club put its stamps on public show for the first time: forty frames on borrowed easels in a church hall, open for one evening. About two hundred people came, mostly to get out of the rain.

One of them was a young science teacher named Inez Calloway, who had come to look at the minerals and stayed to argue about them. She joined that night. She has missed four meetings since, and can name them.
