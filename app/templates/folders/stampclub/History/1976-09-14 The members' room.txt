# The members' room

In the bicentennial summer of 1976 the Society Library on East Seventy-ninth Street offered the club its members' room on the second Tuesday of each month. The first meeting there was on September 14. Nobody has suggested moving since.

![Declaration of Independence, strip of four, 1976](/_stamps/Declaration of Independence, strip of four.jpg)

The room came with a long table and green lamps, and the club has kept both.
