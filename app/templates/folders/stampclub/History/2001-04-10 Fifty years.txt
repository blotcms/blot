# Fifty years

The club marked its fiftieth anniversary at the April meeting of 2001 with a cake iced to look like a sheet of stamps, perforations and all. It was cut along the perforations, which Leonard Frisch said was the first time he had seen it done cleanly.

Inez Calloway gave the toast, which ran to nine minutes, and set a precedent she has been extending since.
