# The stationer's closes

Mr. Kessler sold the shop on Fulton Street in the summer of 1970, and the club held its last meeting in the back room on June 9. He made a short speech, refused a presentation album, and accepted a framed block of four instead.

The club took the table and the kettle and began six years of wandering: a church hall, a dentist's waiting room, a restaurant that wanted the table back by eight, and a basement nobody liked.
