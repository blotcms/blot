# Typography and layout

Type works best when it is left alone. A page that is easy to read is usually a page that has been given enough margin, a steady line length, and one good size for the body.

{|<} A line of twenty words is plenty.

I like a layout that does not announce itself. The photographs, when they appear, can carry the weight; the text just has to get out of the way.
