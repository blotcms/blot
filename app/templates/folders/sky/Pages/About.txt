# About

This site is a collection of skies. Each image is a square detail crop of the sky from a painting, cropped to the clouds, light and colour with the rest of the painting left out. Each image is named for its painting, and its description gives the artist and the museum that holds it.

## How the crops are made

Each crop starts as a high-resolution photograph of a painting from a museum's collection. A square of sky is cut out of it and the rest is left behind. Here is the [[Twilight in the Wilderness]] crop beside the whole painting, with a box around the part that became the crop.

## Museums

The paintings come from the collections of these museums, whose images of their paintings made the crops possible:

- [Rijksmuseum](https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/), Amsterdam
- [The Metropolitan Museum of Art](https://www.metmuseum.org/), New York
- [J. Paul Getty Museum](https://www.getty.edu/museum/), Los Angeles
- [The Cleveland Museum of Art](https://www.clevelandart.org/)
- [National Gallery of Art](https://www.nga.gov/), Washington
- [Yale Center for British Art](https://britishart.yale.edu/), New Haven
