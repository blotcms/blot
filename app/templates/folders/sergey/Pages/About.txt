# About

Sergey Mikhaylovich Prokudin-Gorsky (Серге́й Миха́йлович Проку́дин-Го́рский) was a Russian chemist and photographer. He is best known for his pioneering work in colour photography and his effort to document early 20th-century Russia. 

Each photograph began as a glass negative, 24 x 9 cm, holding three black-and-white frames of the same scene taken through coloured filters (three-color separation). The three frames are combined to make the colour image.
