Google Docs will not be transferred

If you disconnect Google Drive and switch to another sync method, your Google Docs will not be copied. Google Docs are not regular files, so they will not appear in Dropbox, Git, or iCloud.

Files already on Blot will transfer. Changes you made in Google Drive since sync stopped will not. Download those first if you need them.

If you disconnect Google Drive, your site and folder on Blot will stay the same. Blot won’t access your Google Drive folder, and changes on Google Drive won’t affect your site.
