**What to do**

- **Stuck processes** hold memory and the repository's pack. Find the repository they belong to in the arguments, then check `logs green | grep "Git:"` around the time they started. Killing them is safe, since nothing healthy runs this long.
- **Removed directories** need nothing: the sweep already deleted them. Several in a day means pushes are being cut off, so look for a crash or restart (`investigate-production-container-restarts`).
