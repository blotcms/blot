**What to do**

- Renewal starts at 30 days left and runs daily, so under 25 days means about five attempts have failed.
- Find out why: `grep "auto-ssl: issuing renewal certificate failed" /var/instance-ssd/logs/error.log | grep DOMAIN`
- Usually the customer's DNS is wrong (an extra A record, a proxy, a stale record). Once it is fixed, drop the old certificate: `config/openresty/scripts/purge-expired-ssl.sh DOMAIN`, or `config/openresty/scripts/identify-expiring-certs.sh` for everything expiring within 7 days, or `scripts/blog/reset-ssl-certificate-for-expired-domain.js`.
- Wildcard problems: check `config/openresty/scripts/renew-wildcard-ssl.sh` ran, and reload OpenResty if Redis has a newer certificate than the one being served.
- See the full report without sending email: `NODE_PATH=app node app/scheduler/check-ssl-certificates.js`
