The first frost came late this year, and the garden held on to its colour well into November. I spent most of the weekend clearing the beds, cutting back the last of the dahlias and turning the compost, which had finally gone dark and crumbly.

There is something satisfying about the end of the season. The work is quieter, the light is low and golden, and every tidy bed is a small promise about spring. I have written the seed order on the back of an envelope and, for once, I have not gone overboard.

Next weekend: mend the fence by the shed, and finally find a home for the rhubarb.
