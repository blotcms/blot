Technical details

Hosting and domains
Every site includes hosting and a CDN, a custom domain and SSL.
Respectful image handling
Blot does not compress your original image files or reduce their dimensions. Thumbnails are separate, resized images; they do not replace the original file.
File formats
Images in formats not widely supported on the web, such as TIFF, WebP and AVIF, are converted into PNG when published; the source file in your folder is not modified.
Your files stay yours
Your files always remain in your site’s folder, and Blot offers various export formats, including WordPress.
Moving to Blot
Import your posts from WordPress, Squarespace, Blogger or Are.na. If you get stuck, email support@blot.im.
Open source
Blot’s source code is public, and you can self-host it.

What is Blot?

Blot turns a folder into a website. Files in the folder become posts or pages on your site. Update your site by adding, removing or editing files.

How does this differ from other site builders?

There is no editor to log in to: publishing is just saving a file. You write in the tools you already use, and your files always remain in your folder.

Who should use this?

Writers, photographers, artists, designers, researchers and developers. The templates show what each can make.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You can start with just a Word document or a text file. When you want to, you can use the full power of HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Can I publish my own template?

Yes. You can inspect and edit any template’s HTML, CSS and JavaScript, or write your own. The developer documentation explains how.

Where can I get help with my site?

Email support@blot.im, read the documentation, or browse the questions others have asked.

Can I use this for a client?

Yes. Sync a client’s folder with Dropbox, Git, iCloud or Google Drive, and connect a custom domain under your own or your client’s name.
