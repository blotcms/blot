Tot

Write your posts with Tot

Tot is a small text companion for collecting and editing notes on Mac, iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. Its simple interface, iCloud sync, Markdown support and automatic backups make it useful for drafting posts wherever you are.

How to use Tot with Blot

  1. Create a site and choose how to sync your folder with Blot.
  2. Draft your post in Tot using its rich or plain-text editing tools.
  3. Copy the Markdown version of the note into a file with a .md extension in the folder synced with Blot.
  4. Save the file. Blot will publish it automatically after the folder syncs.

Tot works especially well as a mobile scratchpad: capture an idea on one device, then move the finished Markdown post into your Blot folder from your Mac.
