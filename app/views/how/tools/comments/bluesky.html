Bluesky comments

Show the replies to a Bluesky post as comments at the end of your entries. Enable the plugin on the Services page and add your Bluesky profile URL. Blot then finds the post you made linking to each page and renders its thread. You can pin a specific thread to an entry with a Bluesky metadata field. See the Bluesky comments guide for details.
