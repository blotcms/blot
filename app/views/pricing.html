Annual billing?

Yes, after you have signed up for Blot you can switch your billing interval from once-per-month to once-per-year on the subscription page.

Subscription renewals?

You can cancel your subscription at any point. If you pay for Blot annually, we will email you one week before your subscription is renewed to give you time to cancel or update your payment details. If you forget to cancel, contact us and we'll refund your payment.

Can I self-host Blot?

Yes, Blot’s source code is available under the AGPL license.

Alternatives to Blot?

How do I cancel?

You can cancel your subscription at any point on the account page. You can choose to delete your account immediately, or continue using Blot until the end of your billing period.

Bandwidth and storage limits?

There is a soft limit of a terabyte of bandwidth + storage combined per year, per site. If you exceed this, we will ask you to meet the cost of hosting your site, or we will help you set up Blot on your own server. Note that your site is not affected by Google Drive or Dropbox’s traffic limits because Blot hosts the contents of your folder on its own server.

Payment processing

Blot uses Stripe and PayPal to process subscription payments.

Discounts or coupon codes?

We do not ever run discounts, special offers, or issue coupon codes.

Exporting your site

If you decide to stop using Blot, you can export your site to the Wordpress format, which is useful for migrating a site to another platform.
