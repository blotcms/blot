Can I pay annually?

Yes. After you sign up, you can switch to annual billing on the subscription page.

Is there a free trial?

Your payment is refunded in full automatically if you close your account during your first month.

How do I cancel?

You can cancel on the subscription page at any point. Annual subscribers receive an email one week before renewal.

Blot offers various export formats, including WordPress, and your files also always remain in your site’s folder.

Bandwidth and storage limits?

There is a soft limit of 1 TB of bandwidth and storage combined per year, per site. If you exceed it, we’ll ask you to cover the cost of hosting your site or help you self-host Blot.
